Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP)


A set of communication protocols used for interconnecting network devices on the internet. TCP/IP provides end-to-end data communication by defining how data should be packetized, addressed, transmitted, routed, and received. It is the foundation of the internet and enables different types of devices to communicate with each other regardless of their underlying hardware or software.
